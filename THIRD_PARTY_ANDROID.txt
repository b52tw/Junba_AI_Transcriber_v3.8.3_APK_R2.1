Android third-party components
==============================

1. whisper-android
   Artifact: dev.ffmpegkit-maintained:whisper-android:1.0.0
   Purpose: on-device whisper.cpp transcription.
   Audio: WAV / MP3 / FLAC are decoded and resampled internally by the library.
   License: see the upstream artifact / bundled notices.

2. Kotlin coroutines
   Artifact: org.jetbrains.kotlinx:kotlinx-coroutines-android:1.9.0
   Purpose: background execution around the local Whisper API.

3. Android platform MediaExtractor / MediaCodec
   Purpose: local conversion of common compressed Android audio containers such as
   M4A / AAC / OGG / Opus / MP4 to PCM WAV when needed by the Whisper path.

No separate FFmpegKit audio artifact is packaged in this build.
This intentionally avoids duplicate native C++ runtimes and the previous
FFmpegKitConfig runtime class-loading failure.
